## Remaining Boundaries

The EGL test mocks the driver, not a physical 60/120 FPS restriction. The emulator
checks do not replace vendor-device GPU, microphone, thermal or long-session tests.
No universal fix for all mods, renderer combinations or FPS limits is claimed.
